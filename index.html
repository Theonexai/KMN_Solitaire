How to play

Draw one card from the stock. Build tableau sequences in descending order with alternating colors. Build each foundation by suit from ace to king. Only kings can fill empty columns.

Tap a card or stack, then tap a marked destination. Tap it again or press Escape to cancel. You can also drag cards and sequences. Tap the empty stock to recycle the waste.
